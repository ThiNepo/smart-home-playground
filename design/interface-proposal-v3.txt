Status: approved visual reference. Animated native implementation is now available.

ARCHITECTURE
- Street at the front/bottom; productive garden at the rear/top of the same property.
- Main front entrance opens directly into Living room.
- Living room has direct access to Kitchen and separate access to Hall.
- Hall connects the private/service rooms.
- Bathroom and Laundry are retained.
- Rear garden service door is in Laundry, not Bathroom.
- Garage faces the street with an open vehicle-width threshold and a clear straight driveway.
- Exactly four AC units: Living room, Kitchen, Bedroom, Garage.
- No AC in Bathroom, Laundry, or Hall.

MODULAR DEVICE PRESENTATION
- Garage door position and garage contact reading are shown separately from vehicle occupancy.
- This proposal shows garage door Open, door contact Open, and vehicle Present.
- A simulation control can change car presence independently of the garage door.
- Front door and one Bedroom window are monitored; other openings do not need contact sensors.
- This proposal shows front door Open and Bedroom window Closed.
- Selecting a different device would show its own controls and applied configuration.
- Device /state reports current state and applied configuration; configuration changes use a separate command channel.
- Read and publish intervals are independent.
- Shared example credentials: classroom / A7K2M9. Illustrative only; live credentials were not modified.

TRANSPORT DISCUSSION
- The current demo uses native MQTT/TCP locally and MQTT/WSS through the public HTTP tunnel.
- Python Paho supports both transports.
- Native MQTT with TLS is another option when a directly accessible broker is available.
- Concurrent-classroom capacity has not yet been load-tested.
- No transport change was implemented during this design review.
